# DOE/NQA-1 Procurement Quality Assurance Assessment

**Prepared by:** Taniele Weems Toliver  
**Project Type:** Independent Quality Assurance Portfolio Project  
**Date:** September 2026  

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the practical application of quality assurance, procurement controls, risk-based assessment, documentation review, corrective action, and audit-readiness concepts within a simulated Department of Energy (DOE) nuclear-related project environment.

I selected procurement as the focus because my professional background includes government contracting, procurement, operations, compliance, documentation, and working within established government requirements.

The project evaluates a fictional contractor's procurement and quality processes using concepts informed by:

- DOE Quality Assurance requirements
- 10 CFR Part 830, Subpart A
- ASME NQA-1 quality assurance principles
- Risk-based assessment and corrective-action practices

> **Portfolio Disclaimer:** This is an independent educational portfolio project using fictional records and scenarios. It does not represent professional nuclear auditing experience or work performed on behalf of the U.S. Department of Energy, Bechtel, or any other nuclear contractor.

## Project Objective

The objective of this assessment is to determine whether selected procurement and quality processes provide reasonable assurance that:

1. Procurement requirements are clearly defined and documented.
2. Applicable quality requirements are communicated to suppliers.
3. Suppliers are appropriately evaluated before performing quality-affecting work.
4. Purchased items and services are verified against established requirements.
5. Quality records provide objective evidence that required activities were completed.
6. Conditions adverse to quality are identified and documented.
7. Corrective actions are assigned, tracked, verified, and closed appropriately.
8. Assessment results are used to support continuous improvement.

## Assessment Scope

This simulated assessment will review:

- Procurement planning and documentation
- Supplier qualification and oversight
- Flow-down of applicable quality requirements
- Document and records control
- Verification of purchased items and services
- Identification and documentation of quality issues
- Corrective-action management
- Assessment follow-up and continuous improvement
