## 4. Procurement and Quality Assurance Lifecycle

### Procurement Planning

PR-2026-041 documented the procurement need for four stainless-steel isolation valves and established applicable technical, quality, documentation, inspection, and acceptance requirements.

### Supplier Qualification

Northwest Industrial Components, LLC was evaluated and approved for the applicable quality-affecting procurement scope before purchase-order release.

### Purchase-Order Authorization

PO-2026-1187 incorporated applicable technical, quality, documentation, inspection, material-traceability, and acceptance requirements.

Required procurement reviews and approvals were documented before release.

### Receiving Inspection

Four stainless-steel isolation valves were received and inspected against applicable purchase-order requirements.

Three valves satisfied the applicable receiving requirements and were accepted.

A material-traceability documentation discrepancy affecting one valve was identified during receiving inspection. The affected valve was placed on hold and prevented from release pending evaluation.

### Nonconformance Control

The documentation discrepancy was recorded under NCR-2026-001.

The affected valve remained controlled while the condition was evaluated.

Corrected traceability documentation was subsequently obtained and reviewed. Following verification that the corrected documentation adequately established traceability, the affected valve was accepted and released.

### Corrective Action

CAR-2026-001 documented corrective actions associated with the identified traceability-documentation issue.

Implementation of the corrective actions was verified.

A subsequent simulated quality-affecting shipment was reviewed as part of effectiveness verification, and no similar material-traceability discrepancy was identified.

CAR-2026-001 was closed on October 10, 2026.

### Assessment Observation

OBS-01 was documented as an opportunity to strengthen the corrective-action effectiveness-verification methodology.

Although effectiveness verification was performed, future corrective-action records could be strengthened by establishing more specific measurable effectiveness criteria and a defined post-implementation monitoring or review period in advance.

OBS-01 did not require immediate corrective action and did not prevent assessment closeout.
