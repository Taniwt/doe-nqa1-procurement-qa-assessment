### Observation OBS-01

An opportunity for improvement was identified in the documentation of corrective-action effectiveness verification.

The corrective-action process was adequately demonstrated; however, future corrective-action records could be strengthened by defining measurable effectiveness criteria and a specific post-implementation monitoring or review period.

The observation was documented separately in `Observation_OBS-01.md`.
