This record documents the evaluation of Northwest Industrial Components, LLC (NIC) for inclusion on the CBTS Approved Supplier List for procurement of quality-affecting industrial components.

## 3. Qualification Criteria

The supplier was evaluated using the following criteria:

- Technical capability to provide the specified item
- Quality management practices
- Inspection and testing controls

- Traceability of supplied components
- Nonconforming-item controls
- Corrective-action practices
- Ability to provide required certifications and documentation
- Previous performance history, when available

## 5. Evaluation Method

The qualification evaluation included:

1. Review of the supplier's completed qualification questionnaire.
2. Review of available quality-program documentation.
3. Review of supplier-provided inspection and traceability information.
4. Verification of the supplier's ability to provide required procurement documentation.
5. Evaluation of the supplier's capability against the proposed procurement requirements.

Because this is the supplier's first procurement for CBTS, no internal historical performance data was available.

---

## 6. Qualification Determination

Based on the information reviewed, Northwest Industrial Components, LLC demonstrated sufficient capability to provide the proposed quality-affecting components.

**Qualification Decision:** Approved

**Approved Scope:** Industrial valves, fittings, and related components subject to procurement-specific technical and quality requirements.

**Qualification Limitation:** Initial approval is subject to satisfactory performance on the first quality-affecting procurement. Supplier performance should be reviewed following completion of the initial procurement.

## 8. Follow-Up Requirement

Following completion of the first quality-affecting procurement, CBTS will review supplier performance, including:

- Conformance with technical requirements
- Completeness of required supplier documentation
- Delivery performance
- Receiving-inspection results
- Any identified nonconformances or corrective actions

The results will be used to determine whether the supplier should remain approved for future quality-affecting procurements.
