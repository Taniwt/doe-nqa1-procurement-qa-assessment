# EV-007 — Corrective Action Record

**Organization:** Columbia Basin Technical Services (CBTS)  
**Corrective Action Record:** CAR-2026-001  
**Related NCR:** NCR-2026-001  
**Related Purchase Order:** PO-2026-1187  
**Supplier:** Northwest Industrial Components, LLC  
**Date Initiated:** June 19, 2026  
**Initiated By:** Morgan Reyes, Quality Assurance Representative  
**Corrective Action Status:** Closed  

---

## 1. Issue Requiring Corrective Action

During receiving inspection of stainless-steel isolation valves supplied under Purchase Order PO-2026-1187, a material traceability documentation discrepancy was identified for one valve.

The condition was documented under NCR-2026-001.

The supplier subsequently provided corrected documentation that adequately established traceability to the affected valve, allowing the immediate nonconforming condition to be resolved.

Although the immediate condition was corrected, additional action was considered appropriate to address the underlying documentation-control weakness and reduce the likelihood of recurrence.

---

## 2. Cause Evaluation

The supplier reviewed the documentation discrepancy and determined that the issue resulted from an internal document-package preparation error.

The applicable material record existed; however, the final shipment documentation package did not clearly associate the affected valve identification with the corresponding material record.

The condition was attributed to insufficient verification of traceability-document completeness before shipment release.

**Cause Category:** Documentation-Control / Verification Weakness

---

## 3. Corrective Actions

The following corrective actions were established:

| Action | Responsible Party | Target Completion |
|---|---|---|
| Revise shipment-document review checklist to require verification of component-to-material-record traceability | Supplier Quality Representative | June 25, 2026 |
| Brief applicable supplier personnel on the revised documentation-review requirement | Supplier Quality Representative | June 25, 2026 |
| Require independent verification of traceability documentation for the next quality-affecting shipment to CBTS | Supplier Quality Representative | Next Applicable Shipment |
| Review the next applicable quality-affecting shipment for recurrence of the documentation issue | CBTS Quality Assurance | Following Next Applicable Shipment |

---

## 4. Corrective Action Implementation

On June 25, 2026, Northwest Industrial Components, LLC reported completion of the following actions:

- Shipment-document review checklist revised.
- Traceability-verification step added.
- Applicable personnel briefed on the revised requirement.

Supporting documentation was provided to CBTS Quality Assurance.

On June 26, 2026, CBTS Quality Assurance reviewed the supplier's implementation evidence and determined that the planned corrective actions had been implemented.

**Implementation Verification Result:** Acceptable

---

## 5. Effectiveness Verification

Effectiveness was evaluated using the next simulated quality-affecting shipment from Northwest Industrial Components, LLC.

The subsequent shipment documentation was reviewed to determine whether:

- Component identification was clearly associated with applicable material records.
- Required traceability documentation was complete.
- The documentation package contained sufficient information to support receiving acceptance.
- A similar documentation discrepancy had recurred.

The subsequent shipment contained complete and clearly identifiable material traceability documentation.

No recurrence of the condition documented under NCR-2026-001 was identified.

**Effectiveness Verification Result:** Effective

---

## 6. Corrective Action Closure

Based on implementation verification and review of the subsequent simulated quality-affecting shipment, the corrective action was determined to be effective.

## 7. Assessment Consideration

The corrective-action process demonstrated:

- Identification of the condition requiring corrective action
- Evaluation of the underlying cause
- Defined corrective actions
- Assignment of responsibility
- Verification of implementation
- Effectiveness verification
- Formal closure

The corrective action was determined to be effective based on review of a subsequent simulated quality-affecting shipment.

An opportunity for improvement was identified regarding the structure of the effectiveness-verification process. Future corrective-action records could establish more specific measurable effectiveness criteria and a defined post-implementation monitoring or review period in advance.

This opportunity for improvement is documented separately as **OBS-01**.
