# EV-007 — Corrective Action Record

**Organization:** Columbia Basin Technical Services (CBTS)  
**Corrective Action Record:** CAR-2026-001  
**Related Nonconformance:** NCR-2026-001  
**Related Purchase Order:** PO-2026-1187  
**Supplier:** Northwest Industrial Components, LLC  
**Date Initiated:** September 19, 2026  
**Status:** Closed — Effectiveness Verified

---

## 1. Corrective Action Issue

During receiving inspection under PO-2026-1187, CBTS identified a material traceability documentation discrepancy affecting one stainless-steel isolation valve.

The supplier subsequently provided corrected documentation, allowing the affected valve to be accepted. NCR-2026-001 documented the immediate correction and disposition.

This corrective action record addresses the underlying documentation-control issue and actions intended to reduce the likelihood of recurrence.

---

## 2. Cause Evaluation

The documentation discrepancy was evaluated to determine why the material record could not initially be clearly associated with the affected component.

The evaluation determined that the supplier's documentation review process did not adequately verify that component-identification information was clearly reflected in the documentation package before shipment.

**Identified Cause:** Inadequate final verification of material traceability documentation prior to shipment.

---

## 3. Corrective Actions

The following corrective actions were established:

1. The supplier will verify that material traceability documentation contains sufficient component-identification information before shipment.
2. The supplier will add a traceability-documentation verification step to its final shipping-document review.
3. CBTS procurement personnel will clearly identify required traceability documentation in future quality-affecting purchase orders.
4. CBTS receiving personnel will continue verifying traceability documentation before accepting quality-affecting components.

---

## 4. Responsibility and Due Dates

| Corrective Action | Responsible Party | Due Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implement supplier traceability verification step | Northwest Industrial Components, LLC | September 25, 2026 | Complete |
| Update supplier shipping-document review | Northwest Industrial Components, LLC | September 25, 2026 | Complete |

---

## 5. Implementation Verification

CBTS reviewed the supplier's response and confirmed that a traceability-verification step had been incorporated into the supplier's shipping-document review process.

CBTS also reviewed its procurement documentation practices to ensure material traceability requirements are clearly identified when applicable.

The corrective actions were determined to be implemented as planned.

---

## 6. Effectiveness Review

To evaluate effectiveness, CBTS reviewed the documentation package associated with a subsequent simulated quality-affecting shipment from the supplier.

The review confirmed that:

- Component identification was clearly associated with the applicable material documentation.
- Required traceability information was present.
- Documentation was identifiable to the applicable purchase order.
- No similar traceability discrepancy was identified.

**Effectiveness Determination:** Effective

No additional corrective action was considered necessary.

---

## 7. Closure

Based on implementation and effectiveness verification, CAR-2026-001 was closed.
